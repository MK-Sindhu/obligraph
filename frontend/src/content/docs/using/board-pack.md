The board pack is a dated summary of your compliance position for the Board or the Chief Compliance Officer. Open **Reports** (the **Board pack and exports** page). Everyone in the organisation can view it.

## Period

Pick a period with **From** and **To**, or use a preset: **Quarter to date** (the default), **Last quarter**, **Financial year to date** (from 1 April) or **Last 30 days**. A period can be up to 400 days long.

Most figures show your position now. The period decides which new instruments, filings due and approval decisions are included. The coverage trend reads up to a year of daily snapshots ending on the period's last day.

## Sections

The printable version starts with headline figures (policy coverage, open gaps, critical and high gaps, overdue tasks and pending approvals), followed by:

- **Executive summary**: a short narrative generated from the figures in the pack, such as the change in coverage, the weakest regulator, gaps open for more than 60 days, overdue tasks, approvals past their SLA, missed filings and ownership concentration. It doesn't state anything the data doesn't show.
- **Coverage trend**: policy coverage per day, from the daily snapshots the housekeeping job records. It appears once there are snapshots from at least two days.
- **Coverage by regulator** and **Coverage by topic**.
- **Gap ageing**: open gaps by severity, in 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over-90-day buckets.
- **Critical and high gaps**: up to ten, critical first, with their age in days.
- **New instruments in the period**: instruments issued in the period that created obligations for you, and how many of those are still open gaps.
- **Statutory filings**: filings due in the period that were missed or filed late.
- **Overdue remediation**: up to ten overdue tasks.
- **Ownership**: open obligations by owner, including unassigned ones.
- **Readiness packs**: each pack's obligations and readiness percentage.

The on-screen version groups the same figures slightly differently, and also shows approvals decided in the period and superseded instruments.

## Printing and PDF

- **Print / Save as PDF** opens a printable HTML version in a new window and starts your browser's print dialog. Choose your browser's save-as-PDF option there to get a PDF.
- **Download .html** saves the same page as a file.

If your browser blocks the new window, the HTML file downloads instead. Open it and print it to PDF. The printable page has its own print styles.

Each printable board pack you generate is recorded in the activity log with its period.
