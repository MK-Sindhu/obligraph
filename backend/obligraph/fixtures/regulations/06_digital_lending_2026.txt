All Commercial Banks
All Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs)

Sample Directions - Digital Lending

These directions apply to loans sourced or serviced through digital channels, including through lending service providers.

Regulated entities shall disburse every digital loan directly into the bank account of the borrower, and shall not route disbursements through the account of a lending service provider.

Regulated entities shall provide the borrower a cooling-off period of at least three days during which the borrower may exit the loan by repaying the principal and the proportionate annual percentage rate without any penalty.

Regulated entities shall publish on their website the list of all lending service providers and digital lending apps engaged by them.

Regulated entities shall not access the contacts, media or call logs of the borrower's mobile phone.

Regulated entities shall report every digital lending app deployed by them to the Reserve Bank by December 31, 2026.
