All Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs)

Sample Circular - Scale Based Regulation: Layer-wise Requirements

Under the scale based regulatory framework, NBFCs are placed in four layers according to their size, activity and perceived riskiness. This circular consolidates requirements that differ by layer.

All NBFCs shall put in place a Board-approved policy on the grant of loans to directors, senior officers and their relatives.

NBFCs in the Base Layer shall certify their principal business criteria to the Reserve Bank on an annual basis.

NBFCs in the Middle Layer and above shall appoint a Chief Compliance Officer with a minimum tenure of three years.

NBFCs in the Middle Layer and above shall constitute a Risk Management Committee of the Board.

NBFCs in the Middle Layer and above shall disclose their exposure to sensitive sectors in their annual financial statements.

NBFCs in the Upper Layer shall maintain Common Equity Tier 1 capital of at least nine per cent of risk weighted assets at all times.

NBFCs in the Upper Layer shall be listed on a recognised stock exchange within three years of being identified as an Upper Layer NBFC.

NBFCs in the Upper Layer shall put in place a Board-approved internal capital adequacy assessment process, which shall be reviewed on an annual basis.

NBFCs in the Top Layer shall submit a capital plan to the Reserve Bank on a quarterly basis.

These directions shall come into force with effect from October 1, 2026.
