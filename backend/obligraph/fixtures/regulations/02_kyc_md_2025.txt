Sample Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Directions, 2025

These directions are issued under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 and the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002, and apply to all regulated entities listed above.

Regulated entities shall have a Know Your Customer policy duly approved by the Board of Directors, which shall be reviewed at least once a year.

Regulated entities shall undertake customer due diligence, including identification and verification of the customer and the beneficial owner, before opening an account or commencing an account-based relationship.

Regulated entities shall carry out periodic updation of KYC at least once every two years for high-risk customers, once every eight years for medium-risk customers and once every ten years for low-risk customers.

Regulated entities shall report every suspicious transaction to the Financial Intelligence Unit - India within seven working days of arriving at a conclusion that the transaction is suspicious.

Regulated entities shall maintain all records of transactions and identification documents for a period of at least five years after the business relationship has ended.

Regulated entities shall appoint a Principal Officer and a Designated Director responsible for ensuring compliance with the obligations under these directions, and shall communicate their details to the Financial Intelligence Unit - India.

The status of periodic updation of KYC shall be placed before the Board of Directors on a quarterly basis.

These directions shall come into force with effect from July 1, 2025.
