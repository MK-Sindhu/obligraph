Sample Circular - Amendment to the KYC Directions: Periodic Updation

On a review, it has been decided to amend the Sample Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Directions, OG/SAMPLE/RBI/2025-26/01, as set out below.

Regulated entities shall send at least three advance reminders to the customer, through the customer's registered channels, before the due date for periodic updation of KYC.

Regulated entities shall not freeze or close the account of a customer solely on the ground that periodic updation is pending, without giving the customer at least one month's notice.

Regulated entities shall complete periodic updation within thirty days of receiving the self-declaration from the customer where there is no change in KYC information.

These amendments shall come into force with effect from April 1, 2026.
