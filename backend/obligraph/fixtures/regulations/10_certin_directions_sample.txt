Sample Directions - Cyber Security Incident Reporting

These sample directions are issued under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and apply to service providers, intermediaries, data centres and body corporates.

Every body corporate shall report cyber incidents to CERT-In within six hours of noticing such incidents.

Every body corporate shall connect to the Network Time Protocol servers of the National Informatics Centre or the National Physical Laboratory for the synchronisation of all its system clocks.

Every body corporate shall maintain logs of all its information and communication systems within Indian jurisdiction for a rolling period of one hundred and eighty days.

Every body corporate shall designate a Point of Contact to interface with CERT-In and shall communicate the details to CERT-In.

Every body corporate shall furnish information or provide assistance to CERT-In within the time specified in its order.
