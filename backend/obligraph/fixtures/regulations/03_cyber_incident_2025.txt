Sample Circular - Reporting of Cyber Security Incidents

Cyber incidents at regulated entities can disrupt customer services and erode trust. This circular sets out minimum expectations on incident reporting and cyber resilience.

Regulated entities shall report every cyber security incident to the Reserve Bank and to CERT-In within six hours of noticing the incident.

Regulated entities shall have a cyber crisis management plan approved by the Board of Directors, which shall be tested at least once a year.

Regulated entities shall conduct vulnerability assessment and penetration testing of all critical systems on a quarterly basis and shall remediate critical findings within thirty days.

Regulated entities shall conduct an annual information systems audit through an independent auditor and shall submit the audit report to the Board of Directors.

Regulated entities shall maintain logs of all critical systems for a rolling period of one hundred and eighty days.
