All Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs)
All Managers of Alternative Investment Funds

Sample Circular - Cyber Resilience Framework for Alternative Investment Funds

This circular is issued under the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992.

AIFs shall designate a Chief Information Security Officer responsible for implementing the cyber resilience framework.

AIFs shall report every cyber incident to SEBI within six hours of noticing it.

AIFs shall conduct a cyber audit at least once a year and submit the audit report to SEBI within thirty days of its completion.
