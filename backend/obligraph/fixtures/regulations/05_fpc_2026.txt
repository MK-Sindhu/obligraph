Sample Circular - Revised Fair Practices Code and Penal Charges

This circular is issued in supersession of the Sample Circular - Fair Practices Code, OG/SAMPLE/RBI/2019-20/07, and consolidates the fair practices expected of lenders.

Lenders shall provide a Key Fact Statement to every borrower before the execution of the loan agreement, disclosing the annual percentage rate and all fees and charges.

Lenders shall not capitalise penal charges or add them to the rate of interest, and penal charges shall be reasonable and proportionate to the non-compliance.

Lenders shall not engage recovery agents to contact borrowers before 8:00 a.m. or after 7:00 p.m.

Lenders shall display the name and contact details of the Grievance Redressal Officer prominently on their website and at all branches.

Lenders shall dispose of every customer complaint within thirty days of receipt.

The Board of Directors shall review the compliance with the Fair Practices Code and the functioning of the grievance redressal mechanism at half-yearly intervals.

Lenders shall comply with these directions by June 30, 2026.
