# Demo Finance Ltd - KYC and AML Policy

Sample policy for Obligraph development.

## 1. Approval and review
This policy has been approved by the Board of Directors of Demo Finance Ltd. The policy shall be reviewed by the Board at least once a year.

## 2. Customer due diligence
The company shall undertake customer due diligence, including identification and verification of the customer and the beneficial owner, before opening an account or commencing an account-based relationship.

## 3. Periodic updation
KYC records of customers shall be updated periodically based on the risk category of the customer.

## 4. Suspicious transactions
Suspicious transactions shall be reported to the Financial Intelligence Unit - India within fifteen days.

## 5. Record keeping
The company shall maintain all records of transactions and identification documents for a period of at least five years after the business relationship has ended.

## 6. Principal Officer
The company has appointed a Principal Officer and a Designated Director responsible for ensuring compliance with the obligations under the KYC directions, and their details have been communicated to the Financial Intelligence Unit - India.
