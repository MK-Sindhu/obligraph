# Demo Finance Ltd - Privacy Policy

Sample policy for Obligraph development.

The company gives every Data Principal a notice in clear and plain language before seeking consent, itemising the personal data to be processed and the specified purpose.

A Data Principal may withdraw consent by submitting a written request at any branch.

The company implements reasonable security safeguards, including encryption, access controls and logging, to prevent a personal data breach.

On becoming aware of a personal data breach, the company shall intimate the Board and each affected Data Principal within seven days.

Grievances of a Data Principal about personal data shall be resolved within thirty days.
