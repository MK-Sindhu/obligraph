# Demo Finance Ltd - Fair Practices Code

Sample policy for Obligraph development.

The company shall convey to the borrower the terms and conditions of the loan, including the annualised rate of interest, in writing in a language understood by the borrower.

The company shall not resort to undue harassment of borrowers while recovering loans. Recovery agents shall not contact borrowers before 8:00 a.m. or after 7:00 p.m.

The name and contact details of the Grievance Redressal Officer are displayed prominently on the company website and at all branches.

Customer complaints shall be resolved as early as possible.

Penal interest on overdue amounts will be charged as per the loan agreement.
