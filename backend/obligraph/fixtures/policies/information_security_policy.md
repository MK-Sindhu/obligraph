# Demo Finance Ltd - Information Security Policy

Sample policy for Obligraph development.

## Incident management
All cyber security incidents shall be reported to the Reserve Bank within twenty-four hours of detection.

## Audit
The company shall conduct an annual information systems audit through an independent auditor and shall submit the audit report to the Board of Directors.

## Logging
Logs of all critical systems shall be retained for a rolling period of one hundred and eighty days.

## Testing
Vulnerability assessment of critical systems shall be carried out from time to time.
